# Storylines

The picked film is the one already confirmed. The other two are the alternates left behind.

## Picked — The receipt answers

Device: a question with the input. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
A phone alert asks why a headline happened. The receipt printing out of the phone is the lineage, and PlaceWire is the name stamped on that paper. The viewer feels the headline shrink into a trail they can follow.
First two seconds: the lock screen, the headline already on it, "Indefinitely." in the thread color.
Beats: alert → Why? → 2021 → 2023 → the file at 04:09 → the headline joins the trail → PlaceWire and one closing line.
Look: PlaceWire paper #F4F7F8, ink #1B2428, orange thread #C4552A, Atkinson Hyperlegible, a real phone and a thermal receipt.
Sound: no bed. A notification, printer ticks on each line, one stamp on the name.

## Not picked — Today's paper

Older newspaper editions slide out from behind the front page. Left behind because the opener has to bring PlaceWire in as the answer, and the receipt already does that.

## Not picked — Three wrong answers

Cyber attack, lasts forever, and infrastructure down for good get struck out. Left behind so the 30 seconds can be spent on the lineage rather than on the wrong guesses.
