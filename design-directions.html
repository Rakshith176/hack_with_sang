The Paper Trail

Three directions for a feed-first forensics tool

All three open on the news feed and hand off to the same investigation. Each gives it a different material: A a case file, B a signal board, C a plotted trace sheet. Click a headline inside any frame to watch it move into lineage, and use the tabs for drift and ripple.

A

Docket

The product reads as a court file. Every headline is a filing, every precedent is an exhibit, every mutation is a tracked change. Paper and stamps replace the dark dashboard.

Palette
Type
Newsreader for the record, Courier Prime for anything quoted from a document.
Lineage
A docket history with exhibit labels, and the citing relationship written in the margin between entries.
Drift
Tracked changes: dropped qualifiers listed as struck, new claims marked in red.
Ripple
An exposure schedule, set like a table attached to a filing.

B

Switchboard

An operations board for a room that never closes. The feed is a signal rack, the lineage is a cable run, and every state shows as a lamp. Dark, but warm: slate and brass rather than black and neon.

Palette
Type
Spline Sans throughout, with the mono cut for figures, times, and relationship labels.
Lineage
Two cable runs, technical and regulatory, labelled with the relationship each segment carries, meeting at a junction before the headline.
Drift
The claim on two channels, source and wire, with mutated spans lit and a gain meter scoring the distance.
Ripple
Concentric rings by hop count. Distance from the centre is the number of steps from the incident.

C

Trace sheet

A drafting sheet. The lineage is plotted rather than listed: date runs down the spine, technical and regulatory threads sit on either side, and the headline is where they meet. Serif for reading, grotesque for coordinates.

Palette
Type
Bricolage Grotesque for labels and coordinates, Source Serif 4 for prose.
Lineage
A time spine with two lanes. Each event sits in its thread at its date, wired to the spine; both lanes join at the headline.
Drift
Two texts side by side with the mutation measured on a 0 to 100 ruler.
Ripple
A fork: the incident opens three branches, each exposure marked with its hop depth.

What stays in all three

The feed is the front door. Every claim keeps its source beside it. Solid and dashed lines mean stated and inferred. Each direction only changes the material, so the build can borrow from more than one of them.

Reply with A, B, or C and I'll build the feed, lineage, drift, and ripple in that one. Mixing is fine too, for example B's feed with C's lineage.
