Read today’s news with the trail still attached

The Paper Trail already knows four things about a story: what was originally said, how the telling changed, what came before it, and who it reaches. They currently live in separate tools, and the news itself is a popup. These three directions make today’s list the front door, and open a story into the claim, the history, and a reading note a person can actually use.

One story, not five tools

A person opens the app to read the news. Everything else hangs off the story they tap.

  1. Today. Headlines, who published them, and one plain status: matches the record, the telling changed, people disagree, or not enough yet.
  2. The headline they saw, then a reading note in one or two sentences. The note is marked as a reading note and sits beside the sources.
  3. What changed. The original sentence and the headline, then what was dropped, inflated, or widened.
  4. How we got here. A dated trail, newest first, including quiet stretches where nothing public was filed, and steps that are only a reasonable guess.
  5. Who this reaches. A short list. Each line says whether it is known or only possible.
  6. Check this claim. Runs only when asked. Comes back as a sentence plus the sources it used. It never says “true” or “false”.

Old names, if you still think in them: discover news is Today, lineage is How we got here, drift is What changed, ripple is Who this reaches, interrogation is Check this claim. Drill deeper is just an older date on the same trail.

What would change in the backend

The graph stays the source of truth. The screen needs a reading view derived from it, because the current payload is built for a forensic canvas.

  • A feed row: plain status, one sentence on what changed, and how far back the trail goes.
  • A reading note on the story, written when a headline is analyzed, separate from the drift paragraph.
  • Each history step already exists as a node. The view labels it in plain words: on the record, reported, a reasonable guess, or nothing public filed.
  • Who this reaches already exists as ripple nodes. The view wants a person or system, a consequence, and known or possible.
  • The live check stays its own request. It only runs when someone asks.

Words such as epistemic index and telephone factor stay in the data. They do not appear on screen.
